## Integrated Knowledge

The Ewing proposal represents a transformative business model that addresses multiple market needs while creating profound social impact. At its core, the proposal outlines a voice-operated sales enablement platform designed to democratize access to high-value sales opportunities, particularly for formerly incarcerated individuals who face systemic employment barriers.

The immediate focus centers on launching a summer sales class at Ole Miss that serves as both a revenue generator and proof-of-concept for the broader vision. This educational component directly feeds into a larger ecosystem where students, fractional sales professionals, and underserved populations can all participate in B2B sales activities through a revolutionary voice-first interface.

The financial architecture demonstrates clear paths to profitability with existing contracts, identified revenue sources, and a lean operational model that has already proven successful in ventures like BioLev. The $300k initial investment unlocks development of the core platform while solving immediate cash flow needs for the team.

What makes this proposal particularly compelling is its alignment of profit motives with social justice - specifically addressing the paradox where individuals with marijuana-related felonies cannot participate in the now-legal cannabis industry. By removing traditional barriers like computer literacy requirements and criminal background checks, the platform creates genuine economic opportunities for a systematically excluded population.

The strategic partnerships with Ole Miss, AND Capital, and the Jones/Duhon network provide both credibility and scalable distribution channels that can expand from one university to the entire SEC conference and beyond. This network effect, combined with the platform's technical innovations and proven operator team, positions the venture for significant growth while maintaining its social impact mission.
