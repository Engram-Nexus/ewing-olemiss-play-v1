## Overview
Visual campaign builder and management system enabling business users to create, test, and optimize sales campaigns without technical expertise.

## Technical Requirements

### System Architecture
- **Workflow Engine**: Temporal for campaign orchestration
- **Visual Builder**: React Flow for drag-drop interface
- **A/B Testing**: Statistical engine for experiments
- **Rules Engine**: Drools for campaign logic
- **Analytics**: Real-time campaign performance tracking

### Management Features
- **Drag-Drop Campaign Builder**: Visual workflow creation
- **A/B Testing Framework**: Multivariate testing capability
- **Territory Management**: Geographic and vertical assignment
- **Quota Setting Tools**: Individual and team targets
- **Commission Calculator**: Complex comp plan support
- **Resource Allocation**: AI-optimized lead distribution

### Key Capabilities
- **Template Library**: Pre-built campaign templates
- **Approval Workflows**: Multi-level campaign approval
- **Budget Management**: Cost tracking and ROI analysis
- **Performance Optimization**: ML-based recommendations
- **Integration APIs**: Connect to marketing automation

## Implementation
- **Week 1-2**: Visual builder interface
- **Week 3-4**: Campaign logic and rules
- **Week 5-6**: Analytics and optimization

## Success Metrics
- **Campaign Creation**: <10 minutes for new campaigns
- **Testing Velocity**: 5+ experiments per campaign
- **Optimization Impact**: 30% improvement in conversion
